Figures below come from Google Play app details for `country=us`, `lang=en`, read through `google-play-scraper` at 2026-10-26T01:03:53.080Z (Oct 25, 2026, 6:03:53 PM PT).

Play’s public install field is a lifetime bracket such as “100,000+”. It is not a monthly sales count and it is not revenue. Star totals are ratings, not purchases. Written-review counts are smaller than rating counts. Developer sentences about icon counts are listing copy, not an audit.

No source in this note is a verified sales ranking. Monthly sales and monthly revenue for these packs are **unknown**.

## What this set is not

Free packs with large rating counts were left out on purpose. This note is about paid packs. Editorial “best of” articles are not sales rankings. Third-party install estimates, where they exist, are not Play sales and are not used here.

The five US prices fetched here are $0.79, $0.99, $2.49, $3.49, and $3.49. A finished Luma pack at $2.99 would sit between Outline and the One4Studio packs. That is a positioning choice, not evidence that $2.99 sells.
