# Art direction

Luma uses light, luminous squircles. The aim is a refined, dimensional surface: a soft gradient, a pale glass highlight, and a thin platinum edge. Glass and metal stay on the plate. The symbol itself is a bold silhouette so it still reads at about 48 pixels.

Palette, used as plate pairs and small accents:

- Platinum
- Icy blue
- Mint
- Lavender
- Coral
- Amber

Symbols are original geometric interpretations of the app’s job (a cup, a ticket, a vault, a cart). They are not tracings of Apple artwork, of another icon pack, or of the apps’ trademarks. Where a brand mark is a simple letter or a famous silhouette, the icon uses a different object. The X app is a broadcast mark, not the letter X. Snapchat is a gem, not a ghost. Peacock is a screen, not a bird.

Shared plate lighting is the material system. It is not a second icon. Coverage counts only when the symbol geometry differs.

Masters are SVG at a 192 viewBox. Exports are transparent 192×192 PNGs, which is the xxxhdpi size Outline cites for its own pack. A 48×48 export is the readability check.

`iconback`, `iconmask`, and `iconupon` are launcher fallback resources. They are not app coverage.

The pack mark (`ic_launcher`) is a crescent and a vertical light. It is not the metaPhasic Group logo. That logo was not in the workspace.
