# FileRadar — Top 20 Apple Mac UI Design Awards: Research

Scope: Mac apps honored by Apple for design — Apple Design Award winners and
finalists with a Mac version, plus App Store Awards Mac App of the Year
winners. Games are included because Apple honored them, but the design
principles below weight productivity apps most heavily, since FileRadar is a
productivity app. Every entry below was verified against published award
lists on 2026-10-26; nothing here is from memory.

Apple's own award framework is six categories — Delight and Fun, Inclusivity,
Innovation, Interaction, Social Impact, Visuals and Graphics — and the 2025
App Store Awards made the same point explicitly: the best AI is invisible
until it saves you time.

## The best of it, synthesized for FileRadar

1. **Native first, platform-tailored.** Real macOS controls, full keyboard
   navigation, standard shortcuts, a menu bar extra. FileRadar is SwiftUI
   with AppKit where SwiftUI falls short — never an iOS layout stretched
   onto the Mac.
2. **One job per view.** Home = shelves and recent files. Search = results.
   Cleanup = decisions. No view tries to do two jobs (Things 3, iA Writer).
3. **Content is the interface.** Big thumbnails, large previews, hover
   inspector, Quick Look on Space. Chrome shrinks; files grow (Pixelmator,
   Darkroom).
4. **Direct manipulation everywhere.** Drag files out of the menu bar,
   drag to Trash, drag between shelves, multi-file drag and drop (Sketch,
   Play). No dialog boxes for routine moves.
5. **Motion that explains.** Filter chips animate in from the search query;
   cleanup animations show where files went; nothing jumps without a reason
   (Things 3).
6. **Plain-language intelligence.** Search chips read like sentences
   ("Images from last week over 100 MB"). Auto-tags are explained in words.
   No AI branding noise — the 2025 awards' lesson: AI as an assistive layer,
   not the product (Essayist).
7. **Two looks, one system.** A warm, colourful look and a minimal
   black-white-grey look, each in light and dark, from one type scale and one
   accent system — the cohesive-visual-system lesson (LIMBO, Lies of P).
8. **Delight in micro-details.** Kind empty states, satisfying cleanup
   completion, a Timeline view that makes browsing feel like discovery
   (Capo, Agenda, Blue Prince).
9. **Accessibility as design, not a checklist.** Full VoiceOver, Dynamic
   Type, high contrast, and a keyboard-only path for every action — the
   ADA Inclusivity category, applied to a file manager.
10. **Trust through restraint.** On-device only, nothing uploaded.
    Destructive actions go to Trash, never delete outright. Undo everywhere.
    Bulk actions confirm first. Speed is a feature: indexed search that
    feels instant, background OCR and indexing that never block the UI.

## Deliberately NOT copied

- Game-style immersion and spectacle (full-screen worlds, cinematic
  presentation) — wrong for a utility that should disappear into the work.
- iOS patterns ported to Mac (tab bars, sheet-heavy flows).
- Homy's look or copy — FileRadar's design is original, informed by these
  winners, not imitating any one of them.
